title: self-taught
---

I started my journey into the world of computers and programming many moons ago. I was around 12 when I first remember knowing I wanted to become a software developer. I remember going through HTML/CSS tutorials at the time trying to learn how to make websites or even using the browser devtools to edit websites and showing friends, I was pretending to be a hacker with my very basic knowledge. Little did I know I was nothing more than a script kiddie.

At around 15 I found one of Bjarne Stroustrup's books on C++ development. I stuck with that for a few months but I never ended up completing the book. This is also around the time I first installed and tried to use Linux. Linux was nothing more but a curiosity at the time, I was too preoccupied with skipping class, skateboarding. and getting into trouble much to my parents disappointment. This culminated in me dropping out of my first year of high school and getting into lots of trouble both legally and at home. I had no conception at the time of how I was making life much more difficult for the potential futures I had before me.
