No one in this world has yet explained why and how humans _(and other organisms)_ have qualia, phenomenal consciousness, or subjective experience. Proponents of this problem say it is categorically different from the easy problems since no mechanistic or behavioral explanation could explain the character of an experience, not even in principle.

I wonder if oscillating [resonance](./notes/resonance.md) plays a part...
