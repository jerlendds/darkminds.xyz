Grok is probably one of my favorite words. [Grok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grok) is a relatively new word that was coined by the American writer Robert A. Heinlein in his 1961 science fiction novel *Stranger in a Strange Land*.

---

## **grok** _/grŏk/_

**transitive verb**

1. To understand profoundly through intuition or empathy.

**verb**

1. To have or to have acquired an intuitive understanding of; to know (something) without having to think (such as knowing the number of objects in a collection without needing to count them).
2. To fully and completely understand something in all its details and intricacies.

---

## **grokn't** _/grŏkĭnt/_

**transitive verb**

1. To not understand.

**verb**

1. To have not acquired any intuitive understanding of; to not know (something) even with having to think about it.
2. To fully and completely not understand something
